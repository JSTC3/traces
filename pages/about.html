About

I am a student in cybersecurity. I realised I talk about protecting systems but rarely look at my own data. This journal is the result of one week of paying attention to it. There are no quizzes and no tools here, only observations written down as I went.

A day of traces

A normal day, and what each moment can leave behind:

  • 07:00 - I unlock my phone. Device model, time and network are recorded.
  • 09:00 - I search for something. The query is stored by the search engine.
  • 12:30 - I take a photo. Date, time and maybe location go into the file.
  • 16:00 - I sign up for a free service. My email lands in a new database.
  • 22:00 - I scroll a feed. Every pause tells the platform what I like.

Sources and further reading

  • Have I Been Pwned (haveibeenpwned.com) - check if an email appeared in a data breach.
  • EFF Cover Your Tracks (coveryourtracks.eff.org) - see how trackable your browser is.
  • EXIF metadata - the standard that lets photos store date, device and location.

How this site is built

Three HTML pages, one CSS file and one JavaScript file. The script does three things: it switches between light and dark mode (and remembers the choice), it draws a progress bar while you read, and it answers the form below.
